# Tweet Thread: The Invisible Tax

1/ OpenAI spends $1.35 for every dollar it earns. Microsoft bundles the same AI, spends even more in total, and thrives. The difference is not the technology. It's whether the customer can see the bill.

2/ Salesforce tried to sell AI as an add-on. Changed pricing 3 times in 18 months. Adoption: 5-8%. Microsoft bundled AI into Office 365. Same features. No pricing drama. Net revenue retention: 115%+.

3/ The math is brutal. OpenAI's inference costs grew 68% year-over-year. Every query costs real money. When customers see $0.50 per conversation on a spreadsheet, they either don't adopt or negotiate margins to zero.

4/ Bundlers win 80%+ of the market. Microsoft, Google, Apple, Meta spread AI costs across hundreds of millions of users. Cost per user: invisible. Transparent pricers (OpenAI, Anthropic) get 5-20% in high-value niches.

5/ This is not a pricing problem you can solve with better pricing. It's structural. If you can hide the cost in a bundle, you win mass markets. If customers see the per-query bill, you serve specialists only.
