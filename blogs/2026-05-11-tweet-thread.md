# Tweet Thread: The Real AI Infrastructure Moat

1/ Groq built an AI chip 25-40% faster than Nvidia's GPUs. Nvidia just bought them for $20 billion. But Groq was dying before the acquisition. Why? Having the best chip is no longer enough to survive.

2/ AWS margins jumped from 33% to 37.7%. Google Cloud went from 17.8% to 32.9%. Not because their custom chips are better than Nvidia's, but because they own the entire stack: silicon, cloud, and services.

3/ When you own the full stack, you can subsidize infrastructure to win higher margin businesses. AWS can sell Trainium chips at cost internally because they profit from SageMaker, Bedrock, and consulting services.

4/ Independent chip startups need $500M-$2B per chip generation. But only a few dozen customers buy at that scale. Groq had superior tech but no distribution. SambaNova's valuation collapsed 57%. Cerebras only survived by locking into OpenAI.

5/ The infrastructure layer is consolidating to three hyperscalers plus Nvidia. But that opens the application layer. AI agents, vertical SaaS, industry-specific tools - that's where startups can still win through speed and specialization.
