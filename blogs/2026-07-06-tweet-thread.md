# Tweet Thread: Where Margin Goes When Everything Becomes Commodity

1/ Frontier AI models now beat medical specialists by 18 percentage points. GPT-5.2 scored 91.2% on diagnosis tests, the purpose-built medical AI scored 84.1%. The generalist is killing the specialist. Capability became free.

2/ AWS charges $6.88/hour for an H100 GPU. Spheron charges $2.54 for the exact same chip. That's a 171% markup. But enterprises gladly pay it, and AWS margins are perfectly safe. Here's why:

3/ The $4.34 markup isn't for the GPU. It's for compliance ($1.50-2.00/hr), ecosystem integration ($0.60-1.00/hr), and switching costs ($1.50-2.50/hr). Spheron can't compete because they don't have 10 years of enterprise relationships and regulatory certifications.

4/ SaaS sector dropped 40% on per-seat pricing collapse. But Salesforce hit $800M ARR with agent-based pricing (169% growth). HubSpot hit 2027 margin targets a year early. Execution speed on repricing became the new moat.

5/ The pattern repeats everywhere: vendors selling capability die in 12-18 months. Vendors selling integration (compliance, governance, workflow embedding) survive 3-5 years. Margin didn't disappear. It migrated.
