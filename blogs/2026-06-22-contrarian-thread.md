1/ Here is the strongest case against everything I just argued.

2/ Sales AI at Gong and Chorus shows 15-20% win rate improvements when reps follow coaching suggestions. That's measurable ROI, just on a longer feedback loop. The problem isn't data structure, it's that most companies quit before they accumulate enough behavioral data.

3/ Salesforce spent 18 months building Einstein with clean data pipelines and now claims 25% faster deal cycles for enterprise customers. The difference isn't domain structure. It's investment horizon. Most companies expect 6-month ROI when sales cycles are 12-18 months.

4/ HubSpot's marketing AI shows clear attribution: cost-per-lead drops 30-40% when AI optimizes targeting. The reason CFOs can't measure it is poor tooling, not fundamental unmeasurability. Better integration between marketing spend and revenue tracking solves this.

5/ Both things can be true. The outcome depends on whether you're measuring immediate transactional impact (fintech wins) or cumulative behavioral change over 18-24 months (sales and marketing can win, but require patient capital and disciplined tracking most companies lack).
