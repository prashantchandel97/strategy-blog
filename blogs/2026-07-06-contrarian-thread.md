# Contrarian Thread: The Case Against Integration Moats

1/ Here is the strongest case against everything I just argued.

2/ Integration moats break during platform shifts. IBM owned enterprise lock-in in the 1980s with mainframe compliance and ecosystem. Client-server architecture arrived and none of it mattered. AWS's compliance advantage could expire the same way.

3/ Salesforce's 169% growth isn't defensible execution speed, it's first-mover advantage in a pricing model anyone can copy. HubSpot already did. By 2027, every SaaS vendor will offer outcome-based pricing. The advantage disappears.

4/ And specialized providers can build compliance faster than hyperscalers think. Spheron could get SOC 2 and HIPAA certified in 18 months for $5M. If they serve 1,000 customers, that's $5,000 per customer to erase AWS's $4.34/hour compliance moat permanently.

5/ Both things can be true. Integration wins when technology is stable. Capability wins when the platform shifts. The outcome depends on whether we're in a 5-year deployment cycle or a 2-year platform reinvention.
