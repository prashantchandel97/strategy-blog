# Contrarian Thread: The Case Against Moat Compression

1/ Here is the strongest case against everything I just argued.

2/ Stripe did not match Razorpay in 22 days because moats compress fast. They matched because MCP integration is trivial (a wrapper around existing APIs, maybe 200 lines of code). Distribution moats have ALWAYS moved at platform speed. This is not new, we just notice it now.

3/ Token overhead proves the opposite point. If Claude charges 4.7x more and customers still pay, that is STRONG lock-in, not weak moats. Developers tolerate the tax because switching costs are real: rewriting prompts, retraining muscle memory, risking regressions on production code.

4/ Nvidia revenue-share financing is genius, not defense. They are locking in 2-3 years of guaranteed revenue from every GPU sold, which gives them $50-80B in locked cash flow to fund the next product cycle before hyperscaler custom silicon reaches volume production.

5/ Gartner has predicted the death of SaaS for 15 years. First open source (killed Oracle, right?), then cloud commoditization (killed AWS margins, right?), now AI agents. Enterprise software survives every wave because switching costs compound over time, not compress.

6/ Both things can be true. Moats compress in commoditized layers (payments, coding assistants, compute). Moats strengthen in custody layers (data, compliance, trust). The outcome depends on whether you built a feature or a system of record.
