# Contrarian Thread: Governance Is the New Moat

1/ Here is the strongest case against everything I just argued.

2/ Measurement discipline might be selecting for the wrong projects. The 60%+ ROI winners are automating tasks with clear metrics (customer service, forecasting). The 95% failures might be the actually valuable work — strategy, judgment, relationship-building — that AI cannot do.

3/ Razorpay absorbed the Rs 1,209 crore cost because investors valued regulatory standing. But CLOU excluding NBFCs is policy design, not market failure. If regulators wanted small fintechs to compete, they would change the rules. Governance is not a moat — it's regulatory capture.

4/ And antitrust scrutiny does not mean platforms lose. Microsoft has been under investigation for 30 years. They still dominate. FTC and CMA probes slow bundling down but rarely reverse it. Vertical integration might lose 10-20% value from fairness mandates, not 100%.

5/ Both things can be true. The outcome depends on whether regulators actually enforce interoperability (then bundling loses value) or just impose paperwork costs (then large incumbents win anyway because only they can afford compliance theater).
