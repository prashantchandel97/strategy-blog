## What This Blog Has Argued

### AI Infrastructure & Economics

- 2026-05-11: Full-stack ownership beats better chips. Hyperscalers subsidize infrastructure to win application margins. Independent chip startups dead without captive customers.
- 2026-05-18: Cost visibility determines market structure. Bundlers who hide costs (Microsoft, Google, Apple, Meta) control 80%+ of market. Transparent pricers (OpenAI, Anthropic) capped at 5-20%.
- 2026-06-01: Token consumption growing 10x faster than cost declines. Agentic AI burns 3.2x to 50x more tokens per task. Memory now 64-68% of AI chip costs (was 33% in 2023).
- 2026-06-22: Custom silicon cuts costs 40-50% versus Nvidia. Data-native domains achieve 8-month ROI; unstructured domains stall at 18-24+ months. TSMC 3nm at 100% with demand 3x supply.
- 2026-07-06: GPU costs dropped 63%, but AWS charges 171% markup for compliance and lock-in. Frontier models beat specialized AI by 18 points. Capability became free; integration became everything. Margin migrated from hardware and models to governance and switching costs.
- 2026-07-13: Meta spending $145B on AI infrastructure and custom silicon while deferring India fintech due to capital constraint ($12.4B free cash flow versus $19B capex in Q1 2026). Capital allocation reveals belief that infrastructure ownership and differentiation matter more than market expansion. Stripe betting opposite: models converge, infrastructure commoditizes, moat shifts to metering middleware. TSMC CoWoS capacity expanding 10x (13K to 140K units/month) by end 2026, potentially making custom silicon hedge against solved problem.

### SaaS, Moats & Enterprise Software

- 2026-05-25: 95% of enterprise AI pilots fail from scoping problems, not technology. Measurement discipline separates 60%+ ROI winners from 29% losers. Razorpay Rs 1,209 crore redomiciling (32% of revenue) creates structural moat.
- 2026-06-08: Infrastructure moats last 12-18 months. Measurement moats last 3-7 years. Distribution advantages last indefinitely with measurement parity. Per-seat pricing dead due to AI breaking headcount-to-value link.
- 2026-06-15: Validation scope (gap between where moat proven versus deployed) determines consolidation winners. Moats collapse when context shifts. Google 7.25/10, Kissht 5.25/10, Salesforce 2.75/10 on validation framework.
- 2026-06-22: Enterprise AI deepens in data-native domains (fintech 8-month payback), stalls in unstructured domains (sales 18-24+ months). Data structure beats discipline. Fintech SaaS should trade at 20-25x while horizontal SaaS compresses to 5-7x by 2028.
- 2026-07-06: SaaS sector repriced down 40% on per-seat collapse, but Salesforce (169% growth) and HubSpot (hitting 2027 targets early) re-rating upward via fast outcome-pricing execution. Execution speed became new moat.

### Regulatory Dynamics & Governance

- 2026-06-29: Regulatory moats last 5-10 years, then enforcement cycles shift. Cycle 1 (2024-2026 transparency and licensing) protected Salesforce (Flex Credits) and Razorpay (PA license). Cycle 2 (2027-2028 antitrust) turns 30-40% market share into liability. Accenture lost $240M on outcome pricing undefendable under FTC scrutiny. Moats require solving current cycle while preparing for next.
