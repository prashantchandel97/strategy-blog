## What This Blog Has Argued

### AI Infrastructure & Economics

- 2026-05-11: Full-stack ownership beats better chips. Hyperscalers subsidize infrastructure to win application margins. Independent chip startups dead without captive customers.
- 2026-05-18: Cost visibility determines market structure. Bundlers who hide inference costs (Microsoft, Google, Apple, Meta) will control 80%+ of market. Cost-transparent specialists (OpenAI, Anthropic) capped at 5-20% serving high-value niches.
- 2026-06-01: Token consumption growing 10x faster than cost declines. Agentic AI burns 3.2x to 50x more tokens per task. Memory now 64-68% of AI chip costs (was 33% in 2023). Token tax hits ROI walls before vendor lock-in savings rescue the model.

### SaaS, Moats & Enterprise Software

- 2026-06-08: Every competitive moat is temporary but expires on different schedules. Infrastructure moats (Rambus HBM controllers) last 12-18 months. Measurement moats (Eli Lilly weight loss drug, Zendesk verified resolutions) last 3-7 years. Distribution advantages last indefinitely but only with measurement parity.
- 2026-06-08: SaaS market bifurcating into measurable categories (customer support, credit underwriting, code quality) that survive 3-7 years versus unmeasurable categories (team collaboration, project management) that commoditize in 18-24 months. Per-seat pricing model is dead.
- 2026-06-15: Validation scope determines consolidation winners. Moats validated in one context (Zillow past prices, Intel vertical integration) collapse when deployed to different contexts (Zillow future prediction, Intel versus TSMC outsourcing). Google scores 7.25/10, Kissht 5.25/10, Salesforce 2.75/10 on validation framework.
- 2026-06-22: Enterprise AI adoption won't accelerate broadly, it will deepen sharply in data-native domains (fintech 8-month ROI payback, finance 8-month, operations 12-14 month) and stall in unstructured domains (sales 18-24+ month). Data structure determines ROI, not measurement discipline. Fintech SaaS should trade at 20-25x revenue while horizontal SaaS compresses to 5-7x by 2028.

### Governance, Compliance & Market Structure

- 2026-05-25: 95% of enterprise AI pilots fail from scoping problems, not technology gaps. Measurement discipline separates winners (60%+ ROI) from losers (29% ROI or zero impact).
- 2026-05-25: Regulatory compliance costs create structural moats. Razorpay's Rs 1,209 crore redomiciling expense (32% of annual revenue) insurmountable for smaller competitors. India's CLOU initiative stalled because policy design excludes NBFCs.
- 2026-05-25: Platform owners using architectural control to compete downstream now face automatic antitrust scrutiny (Arm, Microsoft). Vertical integration moats repriced by regulators. If fairness is mandated, lock-in advantage disappears.
