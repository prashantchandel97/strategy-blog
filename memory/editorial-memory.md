## Concepts Already Defined for the Audience

Do NOT re-explain these from scratch. A brief reference is enough.

- **Operating margin** (2026-05-11): revenue after operating costs
- **Full-stack ownership** (2026-05-11): owning silicon + cloud + services as unit
- **Hyperscaler** (2026-05-11): AWS, Google Cloud, Azure
- **Vertical integration as subsidy** (2026-05-11): owning one layer at cost to profit from another
- **Inference vs. training** (2026-05-18): training builds model once, inference runs it millions of times
- **Token** (2026-06-01): unit of text (roughly 4 characters or 0.75 words) AI models process
- **Agentic AI** (2026-06-01): AI performing multi-step tasks autonomously
- **HBM (high-bandwidth memory)** (2026-06-01): now 64-68% of AI chip costs
- **CUDA** (2026-06-01): Nvidia's GPU programming platform, source of vendor lock-in
- **Infrastructure | measurement | distribution moats** (2026-06-08): 12-18 month | 3-7 year | indefinite lifespan
- **Per-seat pricing** (2026-06-08): charging per user, broken by AI automation
- **Outcome-based pricing** (2026-06-08): charging per verified result
- **Validation scope** (2026-06-15): gap between conditions where moat proven versus deployed
- **Data-native work** (2026-06-22): work producing structured, deterministic outcomes
- **Frontier models** (2026-07-06): general-purpose large models (GPT, Claude, Gemini)
- **Switching costs** (2026-07-06): expense of migrating between platforms
- **Enforcement cycles** (2026-06-29): Cycle 1 (transparency) versus Cycle 2 (antitrust)
- **CoWoS** (2026-07-13): TSMC advanced packaging, capacity bottleneck now easing (13K to 140K units/month by end 2026)
- **Free cash flow** (2026-07-13): cash after all operating expenses and capital expenditures
