## Core Thesis
Enterprise AI's "crisis" is fundamentally one of **operational maturity, not pricing architecture**. While 56% of CEOs report zero ROI from 2026 AI spending and companies like Uber burned annual budgets in months, the companies that invested in measurement infrastructure and governance discipline move from 29% to 60%+ ROI-positive. The real repricing pressure comes not from subscription costs being too high, but from CFOs refusing to renew tools they cannot measure value from—which will force vendors to bundle governance and implementation readiness as survival tactics in 2027.

## The Key Tension
Does the enterprise AI revenue problem stem from **broken subscription pricing models** (Researcher's angle: token-based unpredictability and SaaS billing architecture are fundamentally misaligned with AI consumption) or from **missing organizational infrastructure** (Analyst's rebuttal: enterprises failed to build measurement frameworks and governance controls, which no pricing model can fix)? The 12% of CEOs reporting both revenue growth and cost reduction suggests the market is working for disciplined buyers—which means the "crisis" is a capability adoption curve, not a systemic collapse. If true, 2027 will force standardization around governance-first buying patterns rather than wholesale pricing model reform.
