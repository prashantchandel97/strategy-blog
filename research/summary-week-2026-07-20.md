## Core Thesis
AI compresses moat durability from 5+ years to 12-18 months. The pattern: misaligned incentives (vendors monetize consumption; agents bypass it) create vulnerability. The insight: **distribution moats beat regulatory moats, but only for 18-24 months**. Razorpay shipped agentic payments first (February 2026). Stripe matched July 9. That 22-day window proves incumbents move at distribution speed. Stripe owns the default Claude payment integration globally. Razorpay owns India's PA-CB regulatory access. But Stripe can acquire a local partner within months, converting geographic moat to regional specialization. All current defensibility (token overhead, GPU financing, SaaS seats, distribution defaults) are interim positions in 18-month cycles. Assume your moat expires Q2 2027 and build the next defensibility layer (custody, compliance, regulation) now.
