## Core Thesis
SaaS per-seat pricing is undergoing forced structural change due to agentic AI, but not through customer replacement—through compression inside existing relationships. Salesforce and ServiceNow's 120%+ NRR proves they're not being displaced; instead, customers are buying fewer seats while vendors compress margins 10–15 points through hybrid pricing (per-agent, per-action, outcome-based) and absorb rising LLM token costs. Sierra's $15.8B valuation isn't a threat to the incumbents; it's middleware playing on top of them.

## The Key Tension
**Researcher:** Cloudflare's 20% headcount cut despite record revenue + Sierra's $15.8B raise signals customers will abandon per-seat licensing entirely for agent-based alternatives. **Analyst:** Salesforce and ServiceNow's expansion revenue and native agent layers suggest the incumbents are keeping customers and successfully experimenting with hybrid pricing models—Sierra is supplement, not replacement. The real pressure is margin compression (60–70% instead of 75–82%) from token costs, not customer loss.
