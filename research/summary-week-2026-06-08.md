## Core Thesis
The per-seat SaaS pricing model is structurally broken (Atlassian saw revenue +21% while seat count fell in Q1 2026), forcing vendors into usage-based, outcome-based, or hybrid pricing. But consolidation isn't inevitable—the real winners will be companies that can measure and prove ROI to customers, especially vertical SaaS and infrastructure players like Anthropic. Profit pools are shifting, not disappearing.

## The Key Tension
**R argues:** Margin compression (80% → 40-50%) makes standalone mid-market SaaS unsustainable; only mega-platforms (Salesforce, ServiceNow) survive by bundling and owning multiple products.

**A counters:** Snowflake and Datadog prove consumption-based SaaS works at scale (120%+ NRR); the real threat isn't margin compression—it's that AI agents make data portable, eroding switching costs. Winners will be vertical-specific vendors who own measurement, not horizontal platforms. Organizational inertia (CIO reputation risk) keeps enterprise lock-in strong; mid-market will defect faster.
