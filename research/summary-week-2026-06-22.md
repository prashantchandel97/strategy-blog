## Core Thesis
Enterprise AI's real dividing line is not measurement discipline, but **starting-domain advantage combined with data infrastructure readiness.** Companies in back-office verticals (finance, operations) have inherently measurable processes and structured data pipelines that allow AI to deliver 41% returns; companies in customer-facing roles (sales, marketing) lack this foundation regardless of measurement discipline. The market is beginning to price this bifurcation (Citi 30 bps credit spread), but SaaS vendors are deliberately obscuring true AI costs through all-in per-seat pricing rather than transparent consumption models—suggesting measured ROI may not be sustainable if exposed.

## The Key Tension
The Researcher argues measurement discipline is the key differentiator, but the Analyst challenges this: if the 41% outperformance is real, why haven't companies proven it through consumption-based pricing (which would expose true costs)? Instead, ServiceNow moved toward bundling AI into base subscriptions, and hybrid pricing remains rare—implying vendors know transparent AI costs kill adoption. This suggests **measured AI winners may be benefiting from process/data advantages in low-risk domains, not superior discipline**, and that true ROI collapses when exposed transparently.
