Domain: Enterprise AI adoption

TWEET OPTIONS

[A] The Number

8 months:

That's the AI ROI payback period for finance departments (invoice automation, forecasting). Sales teams? 18-24+ months. This isn't about measurement discipline. It's about data structure. Finance work is already numbers. Sales work is relationships that resist standardization.

[B] The Quote

Gartner 2026: Finance teams deploy 3.1 AI projects per 1,000 FTEs. Sales teams deploy 0.8. The gap is not adoption velocity. It's whether your domain's work produces structured, measurable outcomes. You can't measure what you can't structure into data.
(256 chars)

[C] Counter-Narrative

Wall Street thinks enterprise AI will accelerate broadly across all industries. Wrong. Fintech SaaS trades at 11-13x revenue (Stripe $159B, Razorpay 11.5x). Horizontal SaaS trades at 4-8x (Salesforce 8.5x, Zendesk 2.1x). AI adoption bifurcates by domain, not discipline.
(272 chars)

[D] Historical Parallel

Today's AI SaaS bifurcation looks exactly like 2000's ERP vs CRM split. SAP and Oracle won (structured financial data). Siebel struggled (unstructured relationship data). By 2004, ERP traded at 6-8x revenue, CRM at 2-3x. Same dynamic now: data-ready domains win.
(263 chars)

[E] The Number (alternative angle)

46%:

That's the valuation premium vertical SaaS commands over horizontal SaaS in 2026. But it's not being earned equally. Fintech/finance/operations (data-native domains) capture all of it. Sales/marketing SaaS get none. Data structure determines valuation, not vertical focus alone.
