[A] The Number

56% of CEOs got nothing from their 2026 AI spending. Not poor ROI. Nothing. The problem: they bought AI as a product when it's actually infrastructure. You can't budget a commodity input the same way you budget a seat license.

[B] The Number

[C] Counter-Narrative

Everyone thinks enterprise AI is a product problem. It's not. 67% of enterprise AI spending has no measurable outcome attached. The model is broken: you can't sell infrastructure with SaaS pricing and expect CFOs to renew blind.

[D] The Number

88% of AI agent pilots never reach production. Microsoft charges $21/month per user for Copilot, but only 29% of orgs report significant ROI. The next wave isn't more pilots. It's mass consolidation or decoupling to open-source.

[E] Historical Parallel

This looks exactly like 2001's ASP collapse. Companies paid for hosted software they couldn't customize or control, costs ballooned, and enterprises moved back on-prem. Today's AI subscription crisis is following the same arc, just faster.
