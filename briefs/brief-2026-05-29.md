Today's research: Why 95% of AI pilots fail (it's not measurement)
Domain: Enterprise AI adoption
────────────────────────────────────

TWEET OPTIONS

[A] The Number

95% of enterprise AI pilots deliver zero P&L impact.

Not because companies can't measure. They measure carefully.

Because speeding up a broken process just automates the waste. MIT found the 5% that work all fixed their process before deploying AI.

(243 chars)

[B] Historical Parallel

The Solow Paradox redux: In the 1970s, massive IT investment failed to boost productivity because companies automated the wrong work.

Now: 95% of AI pilots fail for the same reason. They make broken processes faster instead of fixing them first.

(250 chars)

[C] Counter-Narrative

Everyone blames "bad AI measurement" for the 56% of CEOs who got nothing from AI spend.

Wrong diagnosis. IBM data: only 25% of AI initiatives hit ROI targets because enterprises deploy AI onto broken upstream processes. The process is the problem.
