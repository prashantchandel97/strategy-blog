Today's research: Why Google pays rivals to keep them alive (AI infrastructure consolidation)
Domain: AI infrastructure economics

TWEET OPTIONS

[A] The Number

$920M: Google pays SpaceX this much per month for GPU compute. Not because Google can't afford its own data centers (it approved $150B in capex). Because the real margin is in custom inference chips (40-65% cost advantage), not owning commodity GPU infrastructure. When your moat requires competitors to stay alive, consolidation has entered its endgame.

[B] Counter-Narrative

Hyperscalers built custom chips to escape Nvidia. Now they outsource GPU infrastructure (Google pays SpaceX $920M/month) while capturing inference margin internally. Amazon custom chips: $20B revenue. The endgame: your biggest customers commoditize you while taking margin upstream.

[C] Historical Parallel

2005: VMware won by letting Dell own servers while it captured orchestration. 2026: Google pays SpaceX $920M/month for GPUs while capturing inference (60%+ cost advantage via TPU). Same playbook. Margin always moves one layer up from commodity infrastructure.
