TWEET OPTIONS

[A] The Number

35-45%: That's the margin PhonePe and Paytm earn on insurance distribution versus Turtlemint's 11-12%. Why? No agent acquisition costs. They embed insurance in UPI, reach 150M users, and sidestep the entire agent network. Distribution networks become cost centers when rails change.

[B] Historical Parallel

Today's insurance agent networks look exactly like travel agents in 2005. Expedia didn't beat them with better agents. It eliminated agents entirely. PhonePe and Paytm are doing the same: 35-45% margins embedding insurance in UPI versus 11-12% managing agent networks. Rails win.

[C] Counter-Narrative

Everyone thinks creator platforms need network effects to win. INFLUISH's 500k creators and 50% MoM growth prove otherwise. The moat is payment settlement infrastructure: tax compliance, multi-currency payouts, transaction history. Switching costs are operational, not social.

[D] The Number

₹30,000-40,000 crore: That's India's creator economy annually. The winners won't be the platforms with the most creators. They'll be the ones owning payment rails. 50-70% of creators earn across YouTube, Instagram, Substack. They need one unified settlement layer. That's the moat.
