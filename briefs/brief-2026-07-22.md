[B] Counter-Narrative

Everyone says agentic AI kills SaaS. But Salesforce just launched outcome-based pricing: you only pay when the agent closes a deal.

That's not retreat. That's repositioning. High-complexity SaaS with data moats (CRM, ERP, HCM) aren't dying. They're bundling AI and raising switching costs.

[C] The Number

20% of the entire SaaS market ($234B out of $300B annually) is migrating off per-user licenses by 2030.

Why: AI agents read APIs directly. They do the work of 5 licensed seats but pay nothing. Salesforce charges per human. An agent that replaces 10 reps costs you zero seats.
