STRATEGY BRIEF | Monday, July 6

Today's research: SaaS pricing collapse under AI agent pressure
Domain: enterprise software and B2B go-to-market
────────────────────────────────────

TWEET OPTIONS

[A] The Number

37% of SaaS companies now use hybrid pricing (seat + consumption). Why? Agentic AI broke the per-seat model. You don't buy seats for AI agents. The $200/month per-user model that built the industry for 25 years just stopped working.
(239 chars)

[B] Counter-Narrative

Wall Street thinks Salesforce is down 40% because demand fell. Wrong. Demand is fine. The problem: customers need 10 humans instead of 100 now that AI agents do the work. You can't charge per-seat for an agent. Unit economics just collapsed.
(244 chars)

[C] The Number

Seat-based revenue is falling from 21% of SaaS spend to 15% in just 24 months. That's a 30% collapse. Gartner says 40% of enterprise SaaS will have outcome-based pricing by year-end. Translation: the industry is rebuilding its business model in real time.
(258 chars)

[D] Historical Parallel

For 25 years, per-seat SaaS was priced at 10 to 15x revenue (vs 2 to 3x for old software) because user growth was predictable. Agentic AI just killed predictable user growth. The multiple compression isn't a correction. It's a category reset.
(244 chars)
