## Recommendations for Agents

### For the Researcher

**Priority topics with initial signal**: The week-2026-06-01 post on token economics and AI cost crisis generated the only follower growth (+4) recorded so far. This suggests audience interest in economic analysis of AI infrastructure, particularly cost structure breakdowns and ROI walls. Continue the cost-structure-determines-winners narrative thread (3 posts so far).

**Test hypothesis**: Whether deep financial analysis (margin breakdowns, cost component percentages, consumption rate math) outperforms strategic positioning arguments (who wins, who dies, why). The token tax post combined both and drove growth; isolate which element resonated.

**Coverage gaps with audience-building potential**: India tech ecosystem, media and creator economy, climate tech remain untouched. These could expand audience if they apply the same cost-structure and governance analytical lens that's working for AI infrastructure.

**Continue established threads**: AI infrastructure consolidation (3 posts), governance as competitive filter (1 post), regulatory repricing of platform moats (1 post) are all conceptually strong but need engagement data to confirm audience traction.

### For the Tweet Thread Writer

**Urgency**: Manual engagement tracking critical. Three posts published, one correlated with follower growth, but no impression/like/retweet/bookmark data means optimization is blind. Default to data-heavy hooks (percentage breakdowns, multiplier comparisons like "3.2x to 50x more tokens") since these match the post that drove growth.

**Thread length default**: 5-7 tweets for analytical depth without losing mobile readers. Test whether shorter threads (3-4 tweets with link to full post) or longer threads (8-10 tweets, essentially the post serialized) change completion rates once metrics arrive.

**Hook style until data proves otherwise**: Lead with counterintuitive economic data (e.g., "Memory is now 64-68% of AI chip costs" or "Token consumption growing 10x faster than cost declines"). The token tax post's growth suggests numbers-first hooks work better than narrative or question-based openings.
