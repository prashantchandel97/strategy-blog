## Recommendations for Agents

### For the Researcher

**Strongest signal so far**: Token economics (2026-06-01) combining specific cost breakdowns (memory 64-68% of chip costs) with consumption multipliers (agentic AI burns 3.2x to 50x more tokens) drove only meaningful growth event. Audience responds to infrastructure cost structure analysis with precise percentages and economic implications.

**Established strength**: Cost-structure-determines-winners framework spans four posts (full-stack ownership, bundlers versus specialists, token tax, data-native bifurcation) and represents blog's most cohesive thread. Economic dissection of infrastructure and SaaS categories appears to be core competence.

**Volatility caution**: Week 2026-06-08 saw first follower loss (-3). Whether driven by topic (measurement moats framework), thread quality, or factors unrelated to content remains unclear without tweet-level metrics. Sample size (6 weeks, 7 posts) too small for confident pattern diagnosis.

**Priority until tweet metrics arrive**: Continue infrastructure economics (memory manufacturers as winners, TSMC capacity constraints, datacenter power costs, CUDA lock-in timing). Test whether economic lens applied to new verticals (India fintech, enterprise M&A dynamics, SaaS valuation bifurcation) expands audience or fragments small base.

### For the Tweet Thread Writer

**Critical gap**: Six posts published, one growth spike, one churn event, but zero tweet-level engagement data. Cannot optimize hook style, thread length, or completion rates. Manual tracking from analytics.twitter.com now urgent after six weeks.

**Default hypothesis until data arrives**: Lead with counterintuitive data and specific percentages. Token economics post used "memory 64-68% of chip costs" and "agentic AI burns 3.2x to 50x more tokens" structure. Data-density-first hooks likely outperform narrative or question opens for this audience, but unconfirmed.

**Thread length**: Default 5-7 tweets to match blog post depth (600-800 words) without mobile fatigue. Test 3-4 tweet summaries versus 8-10 tweet deep dives once completion rate data available.

**Recovery signal**: Week 2026-06-15 recovered to 54 followers after 2026-06-08 churn, suggesting issue temporary rather than structural audience mismatch. Continue current strategy while gathering engagement data.
